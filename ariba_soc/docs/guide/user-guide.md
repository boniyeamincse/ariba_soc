# User Guide

## Welcome to Ariba SOC
Ariba SOC is a premier Security Information and Event Management platform designed to streamline alert triage, escalation, and resolution.

## 1. Navigation
The platform utilizes a top navigation bar with the following core areas:
- **Alerts:** The main table for all incoming security events.
- **Board:** A Kanban-style view for visual alert management.
- **My Alerts:** Alerts specifically assigned to your user account.
- **Dashboard:** High-level metrics and KPIs.
- **Reports:** Generate PDF or CSV reports.
- **Wazuh:** Connect and view Wazuh Agent status.

## 2. Your Profile
Click your avatar in the top right to access your profile. Here you can update your avatar, view your role (`L1`, `L2`, `Manager`, `Admin`), and change your password.

## 3. Notifications
Keep an eye out for the yellow banner at the top of the screen; it alerts you when new critical events require immediate attention.
