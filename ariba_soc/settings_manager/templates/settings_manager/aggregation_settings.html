How Dynamic Aggregation Works:

When Wazuh ingests high-frequency attacks (such as SSH brute force or rapid port scans), the aggregation engine calculates a unique SHA-256 fingerprint based on your active grouping fields. If an open alert exists within your configured sliding window (e.g. {{ agg_setting.window_minutes }} minutes), the engine increments the event counter and updates the last seen timestamp rather than cluttering your database and queues.
